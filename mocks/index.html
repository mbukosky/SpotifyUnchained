SpotifyUnchained — Design Exploration

Pick a direction.

Eight standalone mockups across two rounds, exploring different ways to bring the archive to life. Round 2 keeps the current liquid-audio brand (palette, type, glass) and page skeleton — what changes is the motion language and one signature set piece per mock. Nothing here is wired into the real app yet.
